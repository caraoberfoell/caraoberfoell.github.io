Government · Web application

Government Resources website

Increase the usablility of a large database of government resources

Role
UI Designer
Team

Stewart Roud - Creative Director

Z Ter-Stepanian - UX Designer

Timeline
3 months

Outcome

The new site was delivered to the Department of Defense, tested internally by their team, and well received. The site is live today and was reported that they received an increase in traffic.

The problem

The site's data categories were unclear and obscure. Visitors couldn't tell where to look, which led to complaints to the department, higher customer service costs, and slower adoption of the site. Supporting small businesses was a stated client priority, so that audience was where we focused on adding value.

What I did

I designed the site in collaboration with a UX designer, working with the project managers and creative director. Our focus was helping visitors move from a long list of options to the few that mattered to them.

  • Restructured the homepage to have clear informational heirarchy with familiar categories of resources.
  • Introduced a short survey that provided tailored resource results for small businesses.
  • Designed filters, map views and search so visitors could narrow a long list of organizations and see where they are.
  • Designed for mobile as well as desktop.
