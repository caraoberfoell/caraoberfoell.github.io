Working Principles

Work as a team

I don’t have all the answers, but I will do my darndest to have more than when we started.

Iterate often

The sooner we can find out something isn’t working, the sooner we can move toward something that will.

In service to the goal

The work isn't about proving a design is right. It's about making the product better.

Make it useful

Mounds of artifacts that go unread aren't particularly useful. I’d rather spend that time on solutions we can actually implement.

Behind the Work

I’m Cara Oberfoell. Naturally curious, I’ve spent the last 14 years examining products and workflows to understand how they work and where they can improve. For me, design isn't just about making software look good. It's about making complex interactions feel straightforward, so people can focus on the work rather than the interface.

Outside of work, I'm usually reading a mystery, playing a video game, or working through a puzzle. I enjoy a good challenge, especially when the pieces finally come together.
