Role

Team

Timeline
2 years

Outcome

Increased classroom engagement by leveraging exploration and experimentation in an immersive environment.

The problem

Need to find a new and engaging way to teach difficult biology lessons.

What I did

  • Clear instructions and setup because of users unfamiliar with AR technology
  • Multi-player mode encouraged group interactions and learning
  • The use of kinestehtic learning engaged students in new ways
  • Explore, Pratice and Test modes allowed for a stepped approach to learning
